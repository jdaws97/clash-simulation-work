    What this run establishes

    Every declared army and layout instance is bound to a runtime object. Capacity checks reject excess troops or spells. Unsupported selected resources fail explicitly. Destroyed counted buildings determine the model's stars; walls and traps are excluded. Future actions after complete destruction remain unspent.

    Each input declares its termination convention. Older runs continue until complete destruction or the time limit. Newer runs can end when attacking forces and pending offensive effects are exhausted, leaving later support spells unspent. These end rules are explicit hypotheses; displayed durations are simulation results, not measured game timings.

    The battle input records the selected path, deployment, projectile, area, status and lifecycle assumptions. The modifier audit records each changed statistic. Legend I scales defense weapon damage by 1.2 in these examples; ordinary troop statistics and trap damage remain unchanged. The viewer shows the declared layout, including hidden objects, and recorded simulation states. No replay validation or exhaustive army search has occurred.
