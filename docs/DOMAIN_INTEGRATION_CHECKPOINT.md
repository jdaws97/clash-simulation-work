# Permanent-domain integration checkpoint

This checkpoint combines five isolated domain patches with an initial set of
shared-kernel bindings. Catalog quantities and caller-selected lifecycle policies
remain conditional, uncalibrated models. Module presence is not complete coverage.

## Connected and exercised

- Electro Titan has an explicit attacking extension opt-in in v27+ scenes. Its
  ordinary whip and independently scheduled aura use the shared actor, protection,
  damage settlement and paid inventory. A short full battle at two solver steps
  verifies aura cadence, actual Hall HP loss, one paid troop and rejected missing
  opt-in. Raw aura ownership and old-scope bypasses reject.
- Geared Cannon uses the explicit v26+ burst contract and shared projectiles,
  target selection and rate clocks. Kernel checks at two steps verify four shots
  and the declared post-burst gap. Other geared defenses retain their old scope.
- Earthquake Tower level four uses an explicit v26+ maximum-HP pulse hypothesis.
  Released damage persists after source loss, enters shared health settlement,
  and retains source attribution. Positive representable arrival clocks are
  preserved separately from spatial tolerance. Kernel checks verify actual HP
  loss and source destruction at two steps.
- Explicit X-Bow ammunition is consumed by the shared weapon owner. Initial
  rounds and no-refill behavior are hypotheses; no catalog round count is invented.
- Mighty Yak and Angry Jelly use the supplied domain hooks. Short paid full
  battles at two steps verify owner defeat through ordinary Hall fire, one-time
  rage or separation, and resource identity. Attached Jelly contributes its
  owned pending-release fact to the termination collector.
- Five additional defending Castle troops reuse existing attacking compilers
  with explicit defensive roles: Electro Dragon, Dragon Rider, Ice Golem, Giant
  and Hog Rider. The supported defending registry now has twelve identities.
- Rage Vial, Haste Vial, Vampstache and Frozen Arrow hooks are connected by their
  domain patch. Focused component and compilation checks pass; Vampstache and
  Frozen Arrow wall-breach combinations continue to reject pending shared wall
  launch integration.

## Still unbound or unresolved

The remaining attacking extensions, seven advanced spells, five permanent siege
extensions, Logger and Builder Lightning reset are supplied as domain modules.
Their shared callbacks, generated ownership, geometry, resource and continuation
bindings are incomplete. They must not be promoted from module tests alone.

Missing current child statistics and version conflicts remain explicit. Angry
inheritance through Meteor Golem split/throw/merge requires a lineage hook.
Revive must reconcile its selected fallen hero, death location, spent abilities,
absolute statuses and Phoenix entitlement. No default Bat Town Hall multiplier,
Skeleton armor unlock, spawn cadence or Clone eligibility is inferred.
