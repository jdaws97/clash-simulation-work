# Explicit ranked profile and benchmark applicability

The active ranked scenario selector is `legend_i`. Historical Electro31 and
profile-`none` fixtures remain reproducible; they do not establish the behavior
or strength of an attack under Legend I. Town Hall level never selects a league
or modifier. The full-battle caller must supply the battle modifier policy and
mode rules explicitly. War and CWL remain independently selected scenarios.

The [27 April 2026 official update](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashofclans/blog/release-notes/the-sound-of-clash-update/)
publishes Legend I defense DPS+20%, defending hero HP/DPS+20%, Guardian
HP/DPS+20%, attacking hero HP/DPS-20%, and common/epic equipment reduction0/0.
It also specifies eight battles per day and four-week tournaments. This agrees
with the existing numeric profile; this checkpoint corrects its source URL and
records the source publication date and 2026-10-04 verification date in the paid
battle's role audit. Defense HP is unchanged. Percent-of-maximum-HP damage
continues to require its own explicit scaling policy.

These are published rules, not observations of a current client session. They
do not verify current opponent levels, crafted selection or active seasonal
rotation. If an opponent screenshot does not reveal its crafted identity or
module levels, record them as unknown; never replace an unknown active weapon
with a passive Station and call the resulting evaluation complete.

The full higher-TH example still has a passive Station and profile `none`. Its
assembly, ownership and engineering results do not validate a crafted opponent
or a current Legend I strategy. The selected Cake-A-Pult, Hero Hunter and Hot
Candle paths are separately bound and paid-battle tested under the explicit
contracts in [CRAFTED_SEASON_ACCESS.md](CRAFTED_SEASON_ACCESS.md). Independent
shared-path review is pending, and a representative full-village selected-weapon
evaluation remains a separate gate.

Mechanically eligible family coverage, affordable finite full battles, observed
replay calibration and held-out checks, joint army/equipment/pet/deployment/
ability/spell search, and diverse attack/defense evaluation are distinct gates.
Static loadout JSON and same-level synthetic outcomes satisfy none of those
strategy claims by themselves. No optimum, recommendation, or real-game win rate
follows from this checkpoint. Complete held-out game calibration remains zero.
