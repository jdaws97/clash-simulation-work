# Project-generated army and layout evaluation

The requested recommendations must come from candidates generated by this
project. Creator presets are labelled comparison baselines, not relabelled as
our designs after equipment substitutions. No independently generated winner is
currently established. Existing synthetic layout candidates and successful air
clears do not support a strong defensive-layout recommendation.

1. Finish central correctness and independent rechecks on a pinned integration
   tree. Complete the necessary spell/child/lifecycle bindings with real paid
   battle contracts, or explicitly constrain a candidate to supported mechanics.
   Resolve the exact TH16 Root Rider throughput failure before treating that
   matchup as evaluable. Preserve all original recipe receipts and fingerprints.
2. Generate deterministic legal army candidates for TH16, TH17 and TH18 from
   explicit account levels, capacities, availability and roles. Search troop
   quantities, spell budgets and supported hero/equipment combinations. Record
   generation rules, seed, parent candidate IDs, complete input and code hashes.
   Derive layouts independently from legal per-TH inventory: vary compartments,
   Hall placement, damage coverage, approach paths and trap allocation. Retain
   the full buildable layout; do not label a partial synthetic sector a war base.
3. Use a small declared first-stage budget, then expand promising candidates.
   Predeclare matchup sets, seeds, time bounds and scoring. Score completed
   attacks by stars, destruction and elapsed simulation time; evaluate defenses
   against diverse generated attack families and separate comparison presets.
   Timeouts, unsupported inputs and errors are incomplete evaluations, never
   losses or defensive wins. Store progress and concrete completion receipts.
4. Separate search matchups from held-out synthetic validation matchups. Recheck
   finalists under at least two solver resolutions and declared deployment/policy
   perturbations. Report first consequential trajectory differences as well as
   final outcomes. Penalize fragility and require relevant TH16–18 coverage;
   avoid selecting by a single favorable layout, recipe or seed.
5. Deliver only supported selections with full provenance, build instructions,
   comparison results, uncertainty and excluded matchups. A project-generated
   synthetic winner is an experimental candidate, not a game-validated best army
   or base. Real replay calibration remains a separate missing evidence gate.

This is the next evaluation workflow, not a claim that the search ran or that
the unbound mechanics, slow recipe or calibration gaps have been resolved.
