WHAT IS IMPLEMENTED
Reusable finite precompiled child reserves; live caps count living and pending
children; partial batches use remaining live/reserve capacity; dead identities
are never recycled; exhausted reserve sets stop production. Periodic production
skips frozen due batches while its absolute clock continues. Effective settled
HP loss, rather than requested damage or net healing, drives Yeti thresholds.
Actual-death splitting is once per parent. Queued batches survive source loss.
Blocked releases retain their captured position and continuation ownership.
Generated children launch ordinary PatternCombat weapons independently.

Witch batch, living cap and cadence map skeletonsPerSummon, maxSkeletons and
summonCooldown. Golem uses golemitesSpawned; Hounds use pupsOnOffense, never the
defending quantity; Furnace uses firemitesSpawned and lifetime. Super Witch owns
one live/pending Big Boy. Super Hog has an explicit experimental HP split trigger.
Every child needs a complete synthetic/experimental actor and provenance; current
child numbers are not invented or inferred from a catalog description.

Reusable resource preference with reachable ordinary fallback; deployment cloak;
Super Giant wall-weapon bonus; Wall Breaker preference/one-time attack retirement;
Miner surface/burrow transitions with separate immunity and trap-response flags;
finite Super Minion long shots consumed on release including later fizzles;
moving three-stage Inferno Dragon/Super Miner ramps with target/range reset,
explicit Freeze/protection behavior and unchanged ordinary weapon cooldown.

Bowler/Super Bowler have two/three finite packets along captured launch direction,
live membership and a once-per-recipient launch ledger. Source and primary loss
do not cancel released bounces. Super Archer has a declared segment-footprint
piercing helper. IMPORTANT: piercing membership resolves for the full segment at
terminal travel arrival; continuous first-contact arrow motion is not implemented.
Shared damage receipts capture source identity/team and launch damage/buffs.

Super Wizard compiles to the existing shared chain pattern with catalog
chainDamagePerShot / damagePerShot secondary ratio. Super Dragon compiles to an
explicit shared radial pattern. Geometry/link counts/travel remain experimental.
Existing Titan/Druid compilers are reachable through the domain binding function;
their existing runtimes are unchanged and still require shared battle hooks.

child weapon/state models may only select existing validated runtime families.

are explicitly rejected; the defense worker should consume capabilities with
separate team/role/statistic contracts rather than copy these attack quantities.

such combinations pending explicit clock/state-transfer/identity semantics.
Builder-base and seasonal content are outside this workstream.
