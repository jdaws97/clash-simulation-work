# Paid Sky Wagon with finite generated troops

Base: c10b92ecfcf2706c21b65fb89e558317cab9f015. Sky Wagon is admitted in v27+
with explicit siege experimental opt-in and its complete barrel behavior policy.
The existing task-12 domain runtime now owns the shared carrier, one streamed
attacking CC object, launched damage, and every generated reserve. Troop Launcher
and Battle Drill remain rejected in shared battles; no ordinary Wall Breaker or
underground mechanism is silently substituted.

The catalog's selected carrier row supplies barrel count, generated composition
and troop levels. Every Minion, Balloon and Baby Dragon is declared once with its
source-level ID and barrel index, compiled through its real weapon/lifecycle, and
validated against that finite manifest. Carrier donation availability, donor
technology, receiving Castle cap and one siege deployment remain independently
checked. Generated child levels come from the selected carrier, not attacker
research; they cannot spend own-army or CC housing. Missing children, wrong levels,
multiple owners and player deployment commands reject. The dedicated barrel
pattern cannot be used without its matching carrier runtime.

The shared kernel uses the same streamed CC object for commands, release,
continuation and results. Each barrel captures at most one remaining ordered CC
member. Carrier death/manual termination releases remaining unstaged CC members,
cancels unlaunched generated reserves, and preserves in-flight captures and
packets. Exhaustion retires the carrier once after its finite last launch.
Generated resources have carrier-attributed release/phase receipts; they are
excluded from paid deployed-attacker counts. Shared health/protection settlement
and exact siege boundaries handle the actual impacts and final outcome.

Six bounded family tests exercise donated TH16 carrier ownership, real generated
weapon launches at two maximum steps, manual termination during flight, lethal
same-time carrier loss and paid Freeze protection, prelaunch termination,
exhaustion of all four level1 barrels, single CC spending, raw owner forgeries,
and a positive1e-12 flight whose Hall destruction occurs after launch. HP/damage
scaling in the small fixtures isolates lifecycle outcomes; it is not army strength
evidence. Existing siege, recipe troop and payload tests remain required.

Geometry, range, first release, flight delay, impact radius, child offsets,
displacement and blocked-placement behavior are explicit experimental hypotheses.
