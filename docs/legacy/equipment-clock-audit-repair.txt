Root causes and correction

Poison Lizard treated deadlines within spatial EPSILON as reached. This let a
strictly earlier unrelated event take a pulse before Recall and could expire
or reset a still-live exposure. Owned pulse, refresh and expiry comparisons
are now exact deadline comparisons. A call past an unsettled owned boundary
is rejected, including the immediate next representable float. Same-time
refresh-before-pulse ordering is preserved, and expiry remains exclusive.

Rocket used end-EPSILON to finish despite its [start,end) demands contract.
It now retains demands strictly before end, finishes at end, and rejects a
skipped end. A strictly positive remaining Freeze interval still interrupts;
no time tolerance grants an early thaw. Geometry tolerances are unchanged.

New domain helper equipment_event_clock.advancing_deadline validates positive
finite intervals and finite, strictly advancing sums. It never uses nextafter,
an epsilon bump, rounded buckets or a minimum time quantum. Rocket validates
the derived duration/end before recording activation or publishing state.
Poison validates new exposure expiry and first/next pulse deadlines; an initial
pulse must remain strictly before expiry. Unsupported numeric inputs raise
InputError rather than accepting a zero-duration dash or nonadvancing loop.
